Dragon Seal is an Abjuration-school spell that seals information through two castings performed in a single move.

In our party, there is Rudy, a Wizard who can use this spell.

> “I CAST DRAGON SEAL!” -> NAT 15!

The first seal is successfully created.

> “I CAST DRAGON SEAL!” -> ...NAT 1?!

The second casting fails and leaves behind a cracked seal.
